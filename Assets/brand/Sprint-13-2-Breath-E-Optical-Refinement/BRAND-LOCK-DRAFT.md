---

## Primary Mark

OneulRhythm’s primary brand mark is **Breath Flow**, expressed as Soft Taper geometry refined to **E10**.

It represents:

- Presence  
- Rhythm  
- Flow  
- Continuity  
- Calm  

Core statement:

> One rhythm at a time.

---

## Construction Principle

1. **Single open stroke** — never a closed loop  
2. **One full breath cycle** — inhale → exhale  
3. **Soft taper** — thicker through the cycle body, production-floor end weight  
4. **Horizontal reading** — left-to-right calm  
5. **Soft Geometry** — rounded, gentle, timeless  
6. **No secondary marks** — no dots, leaves, accents, or badges inside the glyph  

---

## Brand DNA

| Keep | Avoid |
|------|-------|
| One rhythm | Infinity / loop |
| One breath | Refresh / loading |
| One current focus | Charts |
| Calm flow | Smile / mountain / sunrise |
| Soft Geometry | Checklist metaphors |

## Safe Area (Concept)

- Maintain clear margin of approximately **14%** of the icon edge.  
- Do not crop stroke terminals.  
- Do not place type, badges, or UI chrome inside the safe area.  

Exact safe-area geometry will be finalized at production lock.

## Minimum Size

| Use | Guidance |
|-----|----------|
| App Store / marketing | Prefer ≥ **60pt** display of the mark |
| System app icon | **29pt** must remain a readable single cycle |
| Below 29pt | Do not use the mark alone |

## Background Usage

| Mode | Field | Mark |
|------|-------|------|
| Day | Soft sage (`#E9EFE9` → `#D7E2D8`) | Deep green (`#2F4A3C`) |
| Night | Deep field (`#2A3530` → `#1A221E`) | Lifted stroke (`#D7E4DB`) |

Avoid busy photography, neon, or high-chroma fields that compete with the stroke.

---

## Do

- Keep one open breath cycle  
- Preserve soft taper character  
- Protect optical center  
- Test at 29 / 60 / 180 / 1024  
- Prefer calm contrast over decoration  

## Don't

- Close the path into a loop or infinity mark  
- Raise amplitude into chart / smile / mountain silhouettes  
- Drop end weight below the production floor  
- Add dots, leaves, progress rings, or checklist cues  
- Stretch, skew, or outline the mark arbitrarily

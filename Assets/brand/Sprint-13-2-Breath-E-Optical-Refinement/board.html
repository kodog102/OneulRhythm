01 · Optical Findings

Study of Sprint 13-1 Breath E before micro moves.

01

Optical Center

Exhale trough pulls visual mass slightly low. The glyph wants a gentle lift so the breath sits nearer the optical middle of the squircle.

02

Curve Tension

The zero-crossing between inhale and exhale can feel slightly kinked. Longer handles quiet the S without flattening the rhythm.

03

Stroke Transition

Soft taper is correct in principle. Mid-to-end contrast can be softened so the stroke feels continuous rather than dramatic.

04

End Weight

Terminals approach too thin for reliable 29pt settings-row clarity. Minimum end width needs a production floor.

05

Breath Rhythm

Inhale peak arrives a little early relative to exhale length. More even timing reads as one calm cycle.

06

Negative Space

Left/right air is nearly even, but a slight inset improves containment inside the rounded square.

07

Small-size Clarity

At 29–60pt, taper detail collapses first. Production candidate must hold silhouette under reduction before chasing large-size elegance.

05 · Brand Lock Draft

First draft only. Does not lock the production asset.

Construction Principle

One open stroke. One full breath cycle (inhale → exhale). Soft taper with a production minimum end weight. Horizontal reading inside a rounded square.

Brand DNA

Presence · Rhythm · Flow · Continuity · Calm. Soft Geometry. “One rhythm at a time.” Philosophy before feature metaphor (ADR-010).

Safe Area (concept)

Keep clear margin ≈ 14% of icon edge. Do not crop terminals. Do not crowd with badges or type.

Minimum Size

Marketing / App Store: prefer ≥ 60pt display. System icon: 29pt must remain a readable single cycle. Below 29pt, do not use the mark alone.

Background Usage

Day: soft sage field, deep green mark. Night: deep field, lifted mark. Avoid busy photography, neon, or high-chroma fields that compete with the stroke.

Color (draft)

Mark ≈ #2F4A3C on light. Mark ≈ #D7E4DB on dark. Field day ≈ #E9EFE9 → #D7E2D8. Field night ≈ #2A3530 → #1A221E.

Do

  • Keep one open cycle
  • Preserve soft taper
  • Protect optical center
  • Test at 29 / 60 / 180

Don't

  • Close the path into a loop
  • Raise amplitude into chart/smile
  • Drop end weight below floor
  • Add dots, leaves, or accents

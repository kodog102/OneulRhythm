# Brand Decision Record — Rejected Finalists

**Sprint:** 13-2  
**Purpose:** Preserve why Breath A, C, and J were not selected for production, so future work does not reopen closed directions.  
**Selected path:** Breath E → E10 Soft Taper Synthesis

---

## Breath A — Baseline

**Status:** Rejected for production  
**Role retained:** Historical family reference

### Why it advanced (Sprint 13-1)

Balanced amplitude, clear breath cycle, calm recognition. Served as the optical baseline for the Breath Flow family.

### Why it was rejected

- Uniform rounded ends lack Soft Taper’s inhale/settle grace  
- Feels competent but less memorable as a long-term brand mark  
- Does not improve optical ending behavior needed for production elegance  

### Decision

Keep A as documentation baseline only. Do not revive as the primary mark.

---

## Breath C — Soft Organic

**Status:** Rejected for production  
**Role retained:** Emotional reference (warmth)

### Why it advanced (Sprint 13-1)

Highest human warmth in the TOP 3. Softer Bézier ease felt alive and calm.

### Why it was rejected

- Irregular handles reduce constructability and repeatability  
- Small-size behavior is less predictable than Soft Taper  
- Organic irregularity risks drift across future redraws and exports  

### Decision

Preserve C’s emotional lesson (human ease) inside E/E10’s smoother handles—without adopting C’s irregular geometry as the master path.

---

## Breath J — Optical Taper

**Status:** Rejected for production  
**Role retained:** Presence reference (small-size strength)

### Why it advanced (Sprint 13-1)

Strong recognition and excellent reduction presence through pronounced mid-weight.

### Why it was rejected

- Mid-weight drama feels styled rather than timeless  
- Reads slightly more “designed effect” than quiet breath  
- Soft Taper achieves presence with less optical flash  

### Decision

Carry J’s lesson (protect end/mid presence under reduction) into E9/E10 end-weight floors—without adopting Optical Taper’s dramatic contrast as the brand mark.

---

## Summary

| Mark | Kept lesson | Not kept |
|------|-------------|----------|
| A | Clear balanced cycle | Uniform ends as final form |
| C | Human softness | Irregular constructability |
| J | Reduction presence | Dramatic optical taper |

**Production draft:** Breath **E10**

These rejections are intentional. Reopening A/C/J as primary candidates requires a new Architect decision and Product Owner approval.

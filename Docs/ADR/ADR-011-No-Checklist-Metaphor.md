# ADR-011 — No Checklist Metaphor

**Status:** Accepted

---

## Context

Many productivity applications use checklist-based metaphors as their primary interaction model.

Checkmarks, completed lists, progress bars, and task counters encourage users to maximize completion.

OneulRhythm follows a different philosophy.

The product is designed to help users focus on the most important rhythm of today rather than accumulating completed tasks.

Checklist-oriented metaphors shift attention toward quantity instead of presence.

---

## Decision

OneulRhythm will not use checklist-based metaphors as part of its primary brand or product identity.

The product should emphasize:

- Today's rhythm
- Presence
- Focus
- Calm
- Continuity

instead of:

- Completion count
- Productivity score
- Task accumulation
- Achievement-driven visuals

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reinforces the brand philosophy.
- Creates a calmer product experience.
- Differentiates OneulRhythm from conventional productivity applications.

### Trade-offs

- Some users may initially expect traditional task-management behaviors.
- The product may require clearer onboarding to communicate its philosophy.

These trade-offs are accepted because maintaining a consistent product identity has higher priority.

---

## Scope

This decision applies to:

- Primary navigation
- Core Today experience
- Marketing visuals
- Brand identity
- Future product design

This ADR does not prohibit checklist interactions when they are functionally necessary.

It prohibits using checklist metaphors as the primary identity of the product.

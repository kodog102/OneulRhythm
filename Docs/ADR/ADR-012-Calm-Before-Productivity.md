# ADR-012 — Calm Before Productivity

**Status:** Accepted

---

## Context

Many productivity products prioritize efficiency, speed, and measurable output.

These values often influence interface design through dense layouts, strong visual hierarchy, notifications, and constant encouragement to complete more work.

OneulRhythm intentionally takes a different approach.

The product is designed to create a calm experience that naturally supports focus.

Productivity is considered a result of a calm mind rather than the primary objective.

---

## Decision

OneulRhythm adopts **Calm Before Productivity** as a core product experience principle.

Product decisions should prioritize:

- Calm
- Presence
- Focus
- Simplicity
- Clarity

before optimizing for:

- Speed
- Efficiency
- Completion
- Output
- Performance metrics

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reinforces a distinctive product identity.
- Creates a more sustainable daily experience.
- Aligns visual design, interaction design, and brand philosophy.

### Trade-offs

- Some interactions may appear less information-dense than conventional productivity applications.
- Certain productivity-oriented users may initially expect more metrics and dashboard-style features.

These trade-offs are accepted because preserving the intended experience has higher priority.

---

## Scope

This decision applies to:

- User experience
- Interface design
- Motion design
- Information hierarchy
- Future feature evaluation

This ADR does not prohibit productivity features.

It requires those features to support a calm experience rather than dominate it.

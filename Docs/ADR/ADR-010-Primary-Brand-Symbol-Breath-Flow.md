# ADR-010 — Primary Brand Symbol: Breath Flow

**Status:** Accepted

---

## Context

OneulRhythm required a primary visual symbol that represents the product philosophy rather than a feature.

Traditional productivity applications frequently use symbols such as:

- Checkmarks
- Checklists
- Calendars
- Targets
- Progress meters

These symbols emphasize completion and productivity.

However, OneulRhythm is built around a different principle.

The product encourages users to focus on today's rhythm instead of maximizing completed tasks.

Therefore, the primary symbol should express presence, rhythm, continuity, and calm rather than accomplishment.

---

## Decision

The official primary brand symbol is **Breath Flow**.

Breath Flow visually represents:

- Presence
- Rhythm
- Flow
- Continuity
- Calm

The symbol exists to communicate the product philosophy expressed by the statement:

> One rhythm at a time.

Breath Flow is a philosophical symbol before it is a visual asset.

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Reinforces the brand philosophy across all touchpoints.
- Distinguishes OneulRhythm from conventional productivity applications.
- Provides a consistent foundation for future visual assets.

### Trade-offs

- The symbol may not immediately communicate task management.
- Additional branding may be required during early user adoption.

These trade-offs are accepted because the product intentionally prioritizes emotional clarity over conventional productivity cues.

---

## Scope

This decision applies to:

- App Icon
- Launch Screen
- Widget identity
- Marketing materials
- Brand assets
- Future visual explorations

This ADR does not define:

- Final icon artwork
- Colors
- Typography
- Animation
- Implementation details

# OneulRhythm Product Principles

## 1. Today's rhythm is more important than today's tasks.

The application exists to help users stay connected with today's rhythm.

---

## 2. Less Input. More Presence.

Users should spend less time managing the application.

The application should quietly remain present throughout the day.

---

## 3. Calm over Complexity.

Reduce decisions.

Reduce visual noise.

Reduce configuration.

---

## 4. One Primary Focus.

Only one rhythm deserves primary attention.

The application decides which rhythm matters most.

---

## 5. Consistency Across Surfaces.

Today Screen

Live Activity

Notifications

Widgets

Apple Watch

All surfaces consume the same schedule.

None of them define scheduling independently.

---

## 6. Simplicity Before Power.

The MVP should solve the common case.

Advanced customization should be introduced only after validating real user demand.

---

## 7. Architecture Serves Experience.

Technology is not the product.

A simple and calm experience is the product.

---

## 8. Calm Before Productivity.

Presence comes before optimization.

The product should feel like a quiet companion for today's rhythm, not a dashboard that pushes throughput.

See also `Docs/ADR/ADR-012-Calm-Before-Productivity.md`.

---

## 9. Visual Breathing Room.

Space is part of the product language.

Generous quiet between elements protects hierarchy and keeps Today from feeling like a checklist or control panel.

---

## 10. Background Supports Emotion.

Atmosphere belongs to the experience.

Surfaces such as Today may use landscape and light to hold mood; background never competes with the primary rhythm.

---

## 11. Empty States Are Intentional Experiences.

Empty is not a missing screen.

First Journey introduces the product once. Normal Empty stays quiet after onboarding. Neither is inferred from routine count alone.

See also `Docs/Architecture/Decisions/DR-015-first-rhythm-onboarding-lifecycle.md`.
